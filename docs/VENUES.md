# Venues for the two papers: a shortlist with timelines

*Drawn up on 30 October 2026 (`docs/METHODS_PLAN.md` §7: two papers). The
drafts are due 7 November (paper A, methods) and 14 November (paper B,
evaluation), so the targets are venues with deadlines from December 2026
on, or none. Dates and charges were read on 30 October from the pages
linked at the end; conference dates move and charges change, so check
the official page before submitting. Article processing charges (APCs)
apply only to gold open access; many institutions have agreements that
waive them.*

## Paper A, the methods paper (materials informatics)

| Venue | Type | Deadline | Fit | Cost and notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| npj Computational Materials (Nature Portfolio) | open-access journal | none (rolling) | best by scope: its aims name materials data mining and high-throughput data generation; the widest materials readership of the list | APC £2,790 / $3,590 / €3,090 for research articles (publisher's page); the fee set at acceptance |
| Digital Discovery (RSC) | open-access journal | none (rolling) | good: machine learning and AI at the intersection of chemistry, materials and biotechnology; a chemistry-leaning readership; NLP is not named in the scope, so a cover letter should place the paper | APC £2,200 from 1 January 2026; waived under RSC institutional agreements and for Research4Life countries |
| Journal of Materials Informatics (OAE) | open-access journal | none (rolling) | close in topic (its 2026 content includes LLM-driven materials knowledge extraction); a young journal with less reach | APC $2,600 at acceptance |
| Machine Learning: Science and Technology (IOP) | open-access journal | none (rolling) | an ML-for-science readership; the evaluation protocol is a plus there | list APC £2,680 / €3,215 / $3,350 (an older page; IOP members 25% off; transformative agreements waive it) |
| Computational Materials Science (Elsevier) | hybrid journal | none (rolling) | the no-fee route (subscription publication); scope is computational materials and data informatics, so text mining is a stretch | gold OA USD 3,200, or no charge under the subscription route |
| Winter AI for Materials & Chemistry workshop, Cambridge MA (Microsoft Research New England) | one-day workshop, talks and posters, no proceedings | abstract and registration by 19 November 2026, 6 pm ET; the workshop on 3 December 2026 (MRS Fall week) | an early talk or poster of paper A before the journal submission; free, in person | no publication, so it does not pre-empt a journal |
| AI4Mat (the AI for Accelerated Materials Design workshop at NeurIPS) | ML workshop with proceedings-style papers | the 2026 deadline passed (29 August 2026; the workshop is 12 December 2026 in Sydney); the 2027 call is expected around August 2027 | the natural ML-workshop venue for the method, a year out | free with conference registration |

Recommendation for A: a journal submission to npj Computational
Materials once the draft has had its second pass (or to Digital
Discovery if the user's institution has an RSC agreement), and the
Winter AI4MC abstract by 19 November if the user can be in Cambridge on
3 December. AI4Mat 2027 is the fallback for the ML audience.

## Paper B, the evaluation paper (NLP)

| Venue | Type | Deadline | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACL 2027 (Kyoto) through ACL Rolling Review | main conference, or Findings | ARR submission 4 January 2027 (the ACL commitment date is still to be announced) | the first main-conference window after the drafts; a long paper (8 pages) for the full study, or a short paper (4 pages) for the gap and its anatomy | Findings of ACL takes papers the main conference cannot fit |
| EMNLP 2027 | main conference, or Findings | an ARR cycle around late May 2027 (trackers say 28 or 29 May; the official call is not out) | the next window if January is too early | dates and venue to be announced |
| TACL | journal (ACL) | rolling, the 1st of every month, 23:59 Honolulu time | a journal-length evaluation study with three corpora and pre-registered protocols fits TACL's form; accepted papers may be presented at ACL or EMNLP | TACL warned in July 2026 of review delays from submission volume |
| Insights from Negative Results in NLP | workshop (short papers, 4 pages; non-archival abstracts) | the 7th edition is at EMNLP 2026 (29 October 2026; passed); the 2027 edition is not announced, likely at EMNLP 2027 with a deadline around mid-2027 | the best fit for the negative results and the attempt counts | archival or non-archival |
| Scholarly Document Processing (SDP) workshop | workshop at an ACL conference | the 5th edition was at ACL 2025 (deadline 15 March 2025); the 2027 edition is not announced, past deadlines fell in March to May | NER on scientific documents is its topic | moves between conferences yearly |
| NAACL 2027 and COLING 2027 | main conferences | passed: ARR 12 October 2026, commitment 23 December 2026 | — | only for papers already in that ARR cycle |
| EACL 2027 (Athens) | main conference | passed: ARR 3 August 2026, commitment 11 October 2026 | — | — |

Recommendation for B: the ARR January 2027 cycle (4 January 2027)
targeting ACL 2027 with Findings as the fallback; the Insights workshop
in 2027 if the review asks for a shorter negative-results paper; TACL if
the paper grows past a conference's length.
